Meet BioGen AI Society

Science is better
when we connect.

BioGen AI Society brings together students interested in how AI can help us understand living systems, molecules and the world around us. We’ll learn through approachable workshops, thoughtful discussions and small team projects.

Bring a biology question, a chemistry obsession or your first line of code. You don’t need to be an expert to contribute.

Club in formation · All experience levels welcome

A few things to know

Curiosity is the prerequisite.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Members can contribute through scientific questions, reading, visualisation and discussion as well as coding.

Is this only for biology and chemistry students?

The club is designed for anyone curious about AI and the natural sciences, including students interested in computing, mathematics, physics and interdisciplinary research.

Will there be laboratory experiments?

The initial plan focuses on computational projects, public datasets and discussion. Any future laboratory work would need appropriate institutional supervision and approval.

When and where will meetings happen?

Those details are still to be confirmed. Check the term card for updates.
